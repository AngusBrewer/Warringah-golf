Your phone will ask to use motion. Tap Allow: that's how it feels your swing. Keep this page open while you play.

How to hold it: grip the phone firmly in both hands like a club handle, screen facing the target (the screen is the clubface) and the volume buttons pointing down. Give yourself room to swing.

To swing: tap the green pad, settle at address and hold still until it clicks, then swing. You don't need to keep your thumb on it.

No spinTap the ball: top for topspin, bottom for backspin, sides to curve it. Double-tap to reset.
