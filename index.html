Making a code…
  1. Phone on the same Wi-Fi as this Mac.
  2. Scan the code with the phone's camera and open the link.
  3. Tap Start and allow motion.
  4. On the phone, tap Set full swing and take 3 full, comfortable swings. That sets your 100%.
  5. Pick clubs on the phone and hit some balls. Try the putter too.
